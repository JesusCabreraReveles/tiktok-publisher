Privacy Policy

JCR-PUBLISHER · Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Summary

JCR-PUBLISHER is a personal tool that publishes its owner's videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from other TikTok users and does not sell or share any data.

2. Data the app uses

  • TikTok authorization tokens (access token and refresh token) issued by TikTok when the account holder authorizes the app.
  • Basic account information returned by TikTok (such as the account's open ID and display name), used only to confirm which account the videos are published to.
  • Posting settings returned by TikTok (such as available privacy options), used only to publish correctly.
  • The videos and captions the owner chooses to publish.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account and to check the status of each upload.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored locally on a private device controlled by the owner, in a file readable only by the owner. They are not stored on third-party servers and are not shared with anyone.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to publish content.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is authorized. The account holder can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions); after that, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by its adult owner.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
