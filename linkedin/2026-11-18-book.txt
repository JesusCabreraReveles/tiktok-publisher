When everything pages, nothing gets attention. In IoT and telemetry systems, thousands of devices and sensors can turn a reasonable alert rule into a stream of noise, and the alert that matters arrives buried among the ones nobody reads anymore.

I wrote "Diagnosing and Preventing Alert Fatigue in IoT & Telemetry Systems: A Field Playbook for Production Systems" for the engineers who live with those pagers.

It covers two jobs:
→ Diagnosing alert fatigue in a system that already has it
→ Preventing it in the systems you build next

It's a field playbook written by a practitioner, aimed at production systems rather than whiteboard designs.
